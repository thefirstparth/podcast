# Prompt for ChatGPT: the show's name (copy everything below the line)

---

Help me name a new podcast I'm making for myself. Use everything you know about me: my tastes, my humour, what I watch, the shows and comedians I like, and how I name things. I want a name with character, not a generic sports-podcast name.

The show
- A daily sports podcast made just for me, about 8 minutes, ready at 1 pm IST every day.
- It covers what I follow: Real Madrid, India men's cricket, Max Verstappen and F1, Carlos Alcaraz and Novak Djokovic, the Golden State Warriors, and Cristiano Ronaldo's big moments, plus the biggest results elsewhere.
- It takes my side. Two hosts: a younger Fan who feels every result out loud, and an older, mature, gravelly Anchor who keeps the facts straight. When Real Madrid lose, it is gutted or angry; when Barcelona lose or draw, it celebrates in a short segment called Rival Watch.
- It is the sibling of my afternoon newspaper, The House of 1400, which goes to press at 14:00 (hence 1400). That paper's editor is T. A. Bhide (a twist on Atmaram Tukaram Bhide from Taarak Mehta Ka Ooltah Chashmah), its illustrator is Bunty Brushwala, and the paper's own podcast is called The House of 1400 too.
- One day other people may make their own version for their teams, so a name that could work for them too is a bonus, but it must fit me first.

Rules
- No real living people's names, and no club's official name or slogan as the whole title (for example not "Hala Madrid").
- It must sound good said out loud at the start of an episode, and be easy to find and spell in a podcast app.
- Short is better: one to four words.

What I want back
Give me 12 names. For each:
1. The name
2. The idea in one line: where it comes from and why it fits me
3. How the hosts would say it at the top of an episode, in one line

Then tell me your top 3 and why.
